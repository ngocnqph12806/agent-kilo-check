## What is SkillSeed?

SkillSeed is a peer-to-peer skill-exchange platform. Teach for 60
minutes, learn for 60 minutes — no cash involved. Every session is paid
in **Seeds**, an internal currency you earn by teaching.

- 1 hour of teaching = **60 Seeds** added to your wallet.
- 1 hour of learning = **60 Seeds** deducted from your wallet.
- Seeds expire 12 months after they're earned.

---

## Your first 5 minutes

1. **Confirm your email** — the link in the welcome email is good for 24 hours.
2. **Finish onboarding** at <https://skillseed.app/onboarding>:
   - 1 skill you can teach (with level + experience)
   - 1 skill you want to learn
   - 2 weekly availability blocks (15–60 min each)
3. **You get 60 free Seeds** as a sign-up bonus — that's one free hour of learning.

---

## How a session works

1. **Book** — pick a teacher, choose a 15/30/45/60-minute slot.
2. **Confirm** — Seeds are held (escrowed) when both sides accept.
3. **Join** — at the scheduled time, click the "Join session" button. The Daily.co room opens in-app.
4. **End** — when the timer hits 0 the room closes, Seeds settle into the teacher's wallet.
5. **Rate** — both sides leave a rating within 48 hours. No review = 4★ auto-rate.

If you no-show or cancel < 24 h before, you lose half the Seeds.

2. **Be kind.** SkillSeed is small. We rely on every member being respectful, especially when learning together.
